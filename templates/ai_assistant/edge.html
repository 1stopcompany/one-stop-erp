What this page is: EDGE (from the IFC / World Bank Group) certifies buildings that use at least 20% less energy, water and material-embodied energy than a local reference building. The savings are calculated inside the official EDGE app, not here, and nothing on this page is an EDGE result. This page gathers the design facts that app asks for from the documents you analyzed with the AI Assistant, and shows which ones are still missing, so you can ask the designer for them before entering the project in the EDGE app.
