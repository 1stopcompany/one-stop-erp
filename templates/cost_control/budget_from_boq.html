Budgets come from the project's BOQ

Nothing is entered in Cost Control. A project's quantities and prices live once, in its BOQ (Manage BOQ, from the project page), on the phase or on its sub-items — budget (cost) price and contract (sell) price side by side. Cost Control reads them from there.

  1. Open the project's Manage BOQ and price each phase, or its sub-items.
  2. When buying material, choose the BOQ item the purchase is charged to, on the requisition or order line.
  3. Follow it here under Budget vs Actual.
